# Laws sources review: fundamental rights, arrest, bail, legal aid (2026-10-10)

All URLs below were opened on **2026-10-10** (date accessed). Every quote inside `The law says: "..."` was copied from the source named. Cuts are marked `...`. Where the source was a scanned PDF (no text layer), the quotes were typed from the page images. A lawyer should check those quotes against an official copy.

## constitution-fundamental-rights.md

- https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/nig164561.pdf (PLAC edition: "The Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999, Updated with the 1st, 2nd, 3rd Alterations (2010), 4th Alteration (2017) and 5th Alteration (2023)", printed by PLAC, original print 2019, revised April 2023; hosted on FAOLEX)
- Related: https://placng.org/i/documents/constitution-of-the-federal-republic-of-nigeria-1999/ (PLAC page; it links only the Fifth Alteration Act 2023, not the consolidated edition)

Version: 1999 Constitution as amended up to the 5th Alteration (2023), Chapter IV, sections 33 to 46. The quotes were checked by script against the PDF's text layer and all of them matched.

Double-check:
- This is PLAC's consolidated copy, not a Government Printer / National Assembly copy. Compare with an official gazetted text if one is available.
- Check whether any constitution alteration passed after April 2023 changed Chapter IV. None was checked.
- Section 35(7)(a) (capital offences are excluded from the time limits in s.35(4)) and section 45 (derogations) are summarised. Check that the summaries are not misleading for voice answers.

## police-act-2020-arrest-and-bail.md

- https://placng.org/i/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Police-Act-2020.pdf (PLAC copy, titled "Nigeria Police Act, 2020", 57 pages, scanned, no text layer)

Version: Nigeria Police Act, 2020 (repeals Police Act Cap. P19 LFN 2004). Sections used: 5, 32-38, 43, 44, 46, 48-52, 54, 56, 60-64, 68, 70, 89, 131, 133-135.

Double-check:
- **The quotes were typed from page images** (pages 7 and 17-36, 50-51 of the PDF). Check each quote against an official or gazetted copy.
- The PDF has no gazette page or assent date. Check that it is the final assented text.
- Check for any Police (Amendment) Act passed after 2020. None was checked.
- In the body of the PLAC copy, sections 131-134 sit under "PART XV - POLICE PUBLIC COMPLAINTS AND DISCIPLINE", but the arrangement of sections calls it Part XVI. The file mentions this.
- Section 50(1) refers to "the powers under section 51". From context this seems to mean the stop-and-search power in section 49. It is quoted as printed. A lawyer should confirm it.
- Source typos are kept inside quotes: s.61(2) "have been ascertain", s.62(3) "as the case may,", s.134 "Commissioner of Police a State", s.135 "made under to this Act".
- Section 43(3) says communication or legal advice "shall be done or given in the presence of an officer". This is quoted as printed. Check how it should be explained alongside the right to consult a lawyer.
- Left out to keep the file a reasonable length: s.31 (investigation), s.39-40 (arrest by private persons; the ACJA file has the same rules), s.53 (intimate search), s.58-59 (search warrants), s.65 (summons in daylight), s.137 (unlawful orders).

## acja-2015-arrest-rights.md

- https://placng.org/i/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Administration-of-Criminal-Justice-Act-2015-2.pdf (PLAC copy, 242 pages, scanned, no text layer; linked from https://placng.org/i/documents/administration-of-criminal-justice-act-2015/)
- https://www.policinglaw.info/assets/downloads/2015_Administration_of_Criminal_Justice_Act.pdf (text copy, 292 pages; used to copy the wording)

Version: Administration of Criminal Justice Act, 2015 (original 2015 text). Sections used: 1, 2, 4-10, 14, 15, 17-20, 23, 30-32, 34, 158, 160-163, 165, 167, 168, 293, 295, 296, 349.

Double-check:
- The wording was copied from the policinglaw.info text copy, and the quotes were checked by script against it. Sections 6-10, 18-19, 33-34 and 293-296(2) were also compared by eye with the PLAC scanned copy and matched. Line-break spacing errors in the text layer (for example "broug ht") were fixed.
- **Amendments:** PLAC also lists a bill to amend the ACJA 2015. Check whether any ACJA (Amendment) Act has been passed and changed these sections. This was not verified.
- The ACJA applies to federal offences and the FCT (s.2). Most States have their own ACJ Laws with different section numbers. The file says nothing about State laws because no State law was opened.
- s.19(3) gives **24 hours** to find a suspect's name and address. The Police Act 2020 s.61(3) gives **48 hours**. Both are quoted as printed.
- s.296(4) is not quoted. In the text copy its paragraphs run (a), (b), (d), with no (c). The scanned page was not checked for this.
- Removed while trimming: s.33 (monthly police reports to magistrates) and s.171 (release once the bail bond is signed).

Version: Legal Aid Act, 2011 (repeals Cap. L9 LFN 2004). Sections used: 8, 10, 11, 15, 19, 21. The quotes were typed from page images.

Double-check:
- **Eligibility:** s.10(1) says legal aid goes only to a person "whose income does not exceed the national minimum wage". No naira figure is given, and the current minimum wage was not added because it was not in the sources. In the scanned copy, the section number for "Persons entitled to legal aid" is printed as "6." The arrangement of sections calls it s.10, and the file uses 10. That section also has two subsections numbered (3).
- **Contacts:** all 64 phone numbers in the file were checked by script against the saved official page text. Staff names were left out, and only numbers and addresses were kept. Staff mobile numbers (state coordinators, zonal directors) may change often, so recheck them before launch.
- HQ address: the HQ page says "Off Gimbiya Crescent", but the home page says "Off Gimbiya street". The file gives both.
- Call centre (contact page): +234 7031915990, 0705 905 6923, 0903 950 6089, 0802 574 3195. Telephone: 0903 043 6616. The HQ page gives the same number as 09030436616.
- Small fixes to address text: "!st Floor" became "1st Floor" (Zamfara), "1 ST Floor" became "1st Floor" (Rivers), "Cross Rivers" became "Cross River", "Katsinal Ala" became "Katsina Ala", and "Mal.Yahaya" became "Mal. Yahaya". The Anambra address was changed from capitals to normal case. "Bulding" (Plateau) was left as printed. Yobe has no phone number on the site.
- The website gives no step-by-step application process (forms, documents to bring). The file therefore only says who qualifies, what cases are covered, and where to go. Ask the Council whether it has an official "how to apply" page or leaflet.
- The SERVICOM complaints email (servicomlac@yahoo.com) was not included because it is a Yahoo address.
