NECO (the National Examinations Council) conducts the National Common Entrance Examination (NCEE), the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE), the Senior School Certificate Examination (SSCE) Internal and External, and the National Gifted Examination. NECO says the SSCE External is for candidates who are not in the school system, that is, private candidates. The NCEE is for admission into JSS 1 of Federal Government Unity Colleges, for final-year primary pupils who will be at least 10 years old by September of the exam year.

2026 SSCE External (private candidates). NECO's 2026 registration guideline says the exam is dual mode: a mix of computer-based examination (CBE) and pen and paper examination (PPE), in 16 selected subjects. Fees and dates below are for the 2026 SSCE External only. Confirm on neco.gov.ng before you pay.

How to register for the 2026 SSCE External, according to NECO:
1. Go to the SSCE External link on the NECO website, ssceexternal.neco.gov.ng.
2. Create an account with your email or phone number and activate it.
3. Pay the registration fee into the NECO TSA. NECO lists five payment options: ATM card, internet banking, USSD, wallet and bank branch.
4. Enter your biodata. You must enter a valid National Identification Number (NIN).
5. Go to a reputable cybercafe for biometric (fingerprint) capture and to complete registration. You need a passport photograph not more than six months old, with a white background.
6. Collect the exam timetable and at least one clear copy of your NECO photocard. The referee section of the photocard must be signed and stamped by a school principal, an ordained clergyman or chief imam, a military or police officer not below the rank of captain or its equivalent, a senior civil servant not below GL 14, or a lawyer with at least ten years' experience.
7. Reprint your photocard not later than two weeks before the exam to see your exam centre.

Fees and dates for the 2026 SSCE External, from the NECO guideline:
1. Registration fee: N30,000, including a four-figure mathematical table and a waterproof certificate jacket. Stamp duty, service and Remita charges apply. The fee is not refundable.
2. Registration period: 7 July 2026 to 26 October 2026 at 12.00 midnight.
3. Late registration, 27 October to 3 November 2026: an additional N5,000.
4. Walk-in registration: N43,000 per candidate. Walk-in candidates can register not less than three hours before the subject they want to write.
5. Exam period: Wednesday, 4 November to Saturday, 21 November 2026.

Important rules in the 2026 guideline. Your fingerprints are checked at the exam, and anyone whose photo or fingerprints do not match is treated as an impersonator. Multiple registration is not allowed, and anyone who registers more than once will have all results cancelled. You get one correction only, before the exam starts, and a complete change of name is not allowed. Check all entries a cybercafe makes for you before they upload, because NECO is not liable for registration errors. Phones and other electronic gadgets are not allowed in the exam hall.

How to check your NECO result. NECO says candidates check SSCE and BECE results on NECO Results by buying tokens, and a token can be shared with parents, schools or anyone who needs to see the result. According to the NECO Results Portal:
1. Go to https://results.neco.gov.ng/ and buy a result token (you can create an account to buy and keep tokens).
2. Select your examination year and examination type.
3. Enter your token.
4. Enter your registration number.
5. Click the "Check my result" button.

The portal says each token can be used with only one result, for a specific number of views. For help, email support@neco.gov.ng. If you bought a token from an agent and have a problem, the portal says to contact the agent.

To have your NECO result verified for a school or employer, NECO uses the e-Verify platform at https://everify.neco.gov.ng. You register, buy a verification token, pay through Remita, then submit your exam number. A PDF report is generated and also emailed to the recipient.
