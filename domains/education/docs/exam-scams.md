Nobody can raise or "upgrade" your JAMB score. Ahead of the 2026 UTME, JAMB's Registrar said any claim of score enhancement or examination assistance is fraudulent, and that taking part in such schemes is against the law. He said candidates who pay for these services expose themselves to financial loss and legal consequences. This was reported by Voice of Nigeria.

A real case from 2026. JAMB said a man created a fake identity called "Official Frederick" and ran a WhatsApp group selling score inflation. He deceived at least 94 candidates into paying. The registrations of those candidates were cancelled. After he got bail, he contacted the same candidates again as "Sir Frederick" and asked each of them for N70,000, falsely claiming he could stop the cancellation because he was in contact with JAMB. JAMB said he received the money first in his father's bank account, then in another account opened in the name of a woman he falsely presented as a JAMB director. He was handed over to security agencies.

Fake SMS that look like they come from JAMB. During the 2025 UTME, JAMB said fraudsters joined 8863 (a number form of "UTME") with JAMB's real code 55019 to make a fake sender code. The messages falsely said there were problems with candidates' JAMB details and told them to contact certain people for "help". JAMB said these messages were not from JAMB and candidates should ignore them. JAMB said any message that does not match its official channels, or uses suspicious language or instructions, should be treated as fraud. This was reported by ThisDay.

Paying the right fees, in the right place:
1. JAMB says its UTME and DE registration is cashless. The e-PIN is bought with your profile code from banks and other approved payment partners, and the e-PIN is sent by SMS to your own registered phone number.
2. For the 2026 UTME, JAMB said its fees were N7,200 for UTME without mock, N8,700 with mock, and N5,700 for Direct Entry. It asked candidates who paid more at a CBT centre to report the centre with credible evidence, and warned people to avoid individuals, agents or centres demanding more than the approved rates (reported by BusinessDay).
3. NECO says 2026 SSCE External fees are paid on ssceexternal.neco.gov.ng into the NECO TSA, by ATM card, internet banking, USSD, wallet or bank branch.
4. WAEC result checker e-PINs are sold at WAEC offices and designated e-PIN Mega Dealer outlets.
5. NELFUND says no payment is required before a student loan is paid out.

Protect your details. JAMB tells candidates to keep their registration number, passwords, email address, ATM card details and e-PINs private. JAMB says no centre is allowed to ask for or accept a candidate's password.

Bad staff are also punished. JAMB's Bulletin of 21 September 2026 says JAMB sacked one of its own staff for extorting candidates, and warned that its zero-tolerance policy on corruption applies to everyone.

Rules that cancel results. NECO says anyone who registers more than once for its exam will have all results cancelled, and anyone whose photo or fingerprints do not match at the exam is treated as an impersonator. WAEC says results are cancelled when candidates are found guilty of exam malpractice.

What to do:
1. Do not pay anyone who promises to raise your score, "fix" your registration or release a result.
2. Get information only from the official websites: jamb.gov.ng, waecnigeria.org, neco.gov.ng and nelf.gov.ng. JAMB advises candidates to rely only on its verified platforms and official advertisements.
3. For JAMB problems, raise a ticket on JAMB's online support system, linked from jamb.gov.ng.
