# Education sources review

Date accessed for every URL below: 2026-10-08. Every page was opened and read in
full before any fact was written. Official sites were used first; news pages
are used only where they quote a JAMB statement and no official page had it.

Note: neco.gov.ng, results.neco.gov.ng and the nelf.gov.ng FAQ are JavaScript
apps. Their text was read from the site's own scripts and CMS API
(nelf.gov.ng/api/proxy/cms/api/faqs and /steps), not from plain HTML. Please
open these pages in a browser and check the wording matches what users see.

## jamb-profile-and-utme-registration.md

URLs: https://jamb.gov.ng/FAQ.aspx;
https://jamb.gov.ng/img/2025Advisories/PROFILED_EMAIL_SERVICE1.pdf;
https://jamb.gov.ng/Bulletin/2026/JAMBulletin_21-09-2026.pdf;
https://businessday.ng/education/article/jamb-warns-cbt-centres-against-overcharging/

Double-check:
- The JAMB FAQ still describes the 2025 UTME (fees, age cut-off 30 Sept 2025).
  2026 fees come from BusinessDay quoting a JAMB statement (same amounts); the
  article has no visible date. Replace with the 2027 brochure/advert once JAMB
  publishes it.
- SMS formats copied exactly from the JAMB FAQ: "NIN <11-digit NIN>", RESEND,
  "PASSWORD <email>", UTMEPIN, all to 55019 or 66019. The FAQ says either code
  works for everyone (no DE-only rule found).
- SIM swap / email change (once only, court affidavit) is from the JAMB
  Bulletin of 21 Sept 2026; the fee is not stated there.
- 2027 UTME registration dates and fees: not found on any official page yet.
