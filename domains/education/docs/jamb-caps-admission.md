CAPS means Central Admissions Processing System. JAMB uses CAPS to process all admissions into universities, polytechnics and colleges of education. JAMB says that since the 2018/2019 session, admissions are done only through CAPS. Candidates can check their admission status on the JAMB portal at any time, and CAPS lets candidates confirm (accept) provisional admission offers.

How to check your admission on CAPS:
1. Go to the JAMB e-Facility portal at https://efacility.jamb.gov.ng/. The JAMB website links to it as "CAPS (Candidates)".
2. Log in with the email address and password you used for JAMB registration.
3. Open your CAPS page and look at your admission status.
4. If you have an admission offer, accept or reject it. JAMB's Bulletin of 28 September 2026 advises candidates to promptly accept admission offers made to them.
5. Keep checking. JAMB says your CAPS status will keep changing as the admission process continues, until the final stage where you print your admission letter.

JAMB's Bulletin of 21 September 2026 reports that JAMB explained three statuses that candidates see on CAPS: "Not Admitted", "Admission in Progress" and "Admitted". The CAPS manual for institutions lists the stages inside CAPS as Not Admitted, Proposed, Recommended, Approved and Accepted. Your school proposes and recommends you, JAMB approves, and you accept.

Upload your O'level results. JAMB says candidates who were awaiting results must upload them on the JAMB portal as soon as they are available. JAMB will not consider any recommendation from an institution for a candidate who has not uploaded his or her results. After NECO released the 2026 SSCE results, JAMB advised candidates who were waiting for NECO results to upload or verify them immediately.

Other offers on CAPS: the CAPS manual says that if an institution moves you to a different course (change of programme), you must accept or reject the change before it takes effect. Institutions can also pick candidates from the CAPS "Marketplace". If you are picked, you are told by email and SMS and must accept the offer.

Minimum scores for 2026 admissions. The JAMB website says the 2026 Policy Meeting approved these minimum UTME scores: universities 150; colleges of nursing 150; polytechnics, monotechnics, colleges of agriculture, colleges of education and Innovation Enterprise Institutions 100. The minimum age for admission remains 16 years. The CAPS manual says each school also sets its own cut-off mark on CAPS, and that school does not process candidates below it.

Admission deadlines for the 2026/2027 session, from the JAMB Bulletin of 28 September 2026:
1. Public universities: on or before 31 October 2026.
2. Private universities: on or before 30 November 2026.
3. Other tertiary institutions: on or before 31 December 2026.

The same bulletin says CAPS was down for about 96 hours in September 2026 and was fully restored on Sunday, 27 September 2026.

Fees on JAMB's Payment and Services page (no year is shown, so confirm on jamb.gov.ng): JAMB admission letter N1,000 plus N100 CBT service charge; JAMB result slip N1,000 plus N100; retrieve registration number N1,000 plus N100; correction of data (institution/course, name, date of birth, state/LGA, gender or A'level qualification) N2,500 plus N100 for each item.

For help, JAMB asks candidates to raise a ticket on its online support system, linked from jamb.gov.ng.
