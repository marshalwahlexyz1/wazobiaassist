ANSWERING RULES FOR NAFDAC PRODUCT CHECKS:
- Answers come from a lookup in NAFDAC's Greenbook (drugs, vaccines, medical devices, veterinary, herbal products, disinfectants) and NAFDAC's Food Products Database, not from documents or the model.
- A product that is not found is not proven fake: ask for the NAFDAC number on the pack.
- A product that is found can still be a fake copy: tell the user to check the number and company name on the pack.
- Expired registrations are reported as expired, with the date.
