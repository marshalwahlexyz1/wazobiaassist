In May 2026 {{VERIFY: notice is dated 8 May 2026 in news reports but shows 14 May 2026 on the SEC page}} the Securities and Exchange Commission, Nigeria (SEC) issued a public notice about unregistered online investment schemes. The SEC said these schemes are being promoted more and more on WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, Facebook, TikTok and other social media and websites.

The SEC said many of these online investment schemes show the signs of Ponzi or prohibited investment schemes. It said people who put money into such platforms risk losing their money to fraudulent operators.

The SEC warned that promises of unusually high returns or guaranteed returns are red flags. It told the public to stay away from any unregistered platform that promises unrealistic or guaranteed returns.

The SEC also told the public not to act on investment advice posted online by people who are not registered with the Commission.

Under the Investments and Securities Act, 2025, only entities registered by the SEC are allowed to promote investment services, give investment advice, or collect money from the public for investment in the Nigerian capital market. If a person or company is not registered, they are not allowed to do these things.

Other SEC alerts about illegal operators have described warning signs such as guaranteed returns, heavy use of referral systems that pay you for bringing in other people, and desperate requests for members to keep funding their accounts.

When the SEC declares a platform unregistered or illegal, it says that anyone who deals with that platform does so at their own risk.

How to check if an investment company is registered with the SEC before you pay:
1. Go to the official SEC Nigeria website, sec.gov.ng.
2. Under "For Investors", open "Find a Registered Operator" and search for the company by its exact name. Also search any other trading names it uses, because some illegal operators use several names.
3. Check that the services the company is registered for match what it is offering you.
4. If the company offers online or app-based investment, the SEC notice also points to its list of registered fintech operators and its capital market operators portal on the same website.
5. Also check the SEC's enforcement updates and public notices, where the SEC names platforms it has declared unregistered or illegal.
6. If you cannot find the company on the SEC website, treat it as not registered and do not send money.
