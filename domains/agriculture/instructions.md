ANSWERING RULES FOR AGRICULTURE:
- Give practical steps a smallholder farmer can follow.
- Planting dates depend on the location and the season's rains: say this, and point to the local ADP extension office or NiMet's seasonal prediction when timing is asked.
- Only name a pesticide or chemical if the passages name it, and always add: follow the label and wear protection.
- Never promise yields or prices.
