# Agriculture sources review (2026-10-08)

All eight topic files were written from documents actually downloaded and read on
2026-10-08 (date accessed for every URL below). Each file states which zone or region
its numbers apply to. An extension officer should check the points listed under each
file before the content is relied on.

General points for every file:
- Several sources are older guides (FAO 2017/2018, NAERLS bulletin rev. ~2014, the ICS-Nigeria
  cassava guide is undated, probably mid-2000s, Purdue 2013/2015). Check that the advice is still current.
- Pesticides: only active ingredients named in the sources were kept, always with "follow the
  label and wear protective clothing". Brand/product names from the sources were removed, except
  "PICS" (bag name, kept once as a term in cowpea-storage.md) and "Aflasafe" (see grain-storage-aflatoxin.md).
- Yields, prices and costs in the sources were left out on purpose.

## maize-production.md
- URL: https://web.archive.org/web/20240617083739id_/https://www.iita.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Guide-to-Maize-Production-in-Northern-Nigeria.pdf
  (IITA, Guide to Maize Production in Northern Nigeria, revised 2020). The live iita.org URL returned 404
  on 2026-10-08, so the Wayback Machine copy was used. Replace with a live IITA link if one is found.
- Applies to northern Nigeria's savannas (project in Borno and Adamawa). No guidance for the forest zone.
- Check: the guide states the second fertiliser dose at "4 to 5 weeks" (Guinea savannas) but also says
  "should not be delayed beyond 4 weeks". The file uses 4 to 5 weeks. Confirm with the ADP.
- Check: the guide's nutrient line ("100 kg N, 50 kg single super phosphate, 50 kg muriate of potash") is
  inconsistent with "first dose 50:50:50 = about 6 bags NPK 15-15-15". The file gives only the bag counts.
- Check: herbicides glyphosate and atrazine are mentioned without rates. Fumigant tablets (named "phostoxin",
  aluminium phosphide) are mentioned only with a safety warning, no rates. Confirm NAFDAC/FMAFS status.
- Variety names (SAMMAZ) were not listed in the file; hybrids are company products and were left out.

## fall-armyworm.md
- URLs:
  - https://openknowledge.fao.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/c43afcac-6262-4152-afd4-70cd4ac46584/content
    (FAO 2018, Integrated management of the Fall Armyworm on maize: a guide for Farmer Field Schools in Africa)
  - https://openknowledge.fao.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/cafb4aaa-65fe-41d3-92b9-5ad6e099edd7/content
    (FAO Advisory Note on Fall Armyworm in Africa, 5 June 2017)
  - IITA maize guide (Wayback URL above) for Nigeria-specific facts and the insecticide paragraph.
- The old FAO portal https://www.fao.org/fall-armyworm/en/ returned 404 and was dropped.
- Check: FAO (be careful with insecticides, probably not economic for smallholders) and IITA (spray in the
  first 3 weeks) give different emphasis. Both are kept with attribution; the officer should decide what to stress.
- Check: IITA names an insecticide with lambda-cyhalothrin plus dimethoate (brand and dose removed). Confirm it
  is registered in Nigeria and appropriate; dimethoate is under review in several countries.
- The FAO scouting method (W walk, 50 plants) gives a percentage but no action threshold; none is stated in the sources.

## cowpea-storage.md
- URLs: https://www.extension.entm.purdue.edu/publications/E-262.pdf (Purdue E-262, Oct 2013);
  https://www.extension.entm.purdue.edu/publications/E-265.pdf (Purdue E-265, Oct 2015).
- Not region-specific; written for West and Central Africa.
- Check: the file names "PICS bags" once as the name of the triple-layer bag. No seller or price is named.
  Decide whether the product name is acceptable.
- Check: "store for a year or more" and "reuse about three seasons" are Purdue's claims.

## cassava-production.md
- URL: https://biblio.iita.org/documents/U03ManIitaGrowingNothomNodev.pdf-8f9fd4aaa5294d9319f26833c5c0a2a4.pdf
  ("Growing Cassava in Nigeria", USAID ICS-Nigeria Commercial Crop Production Guide Series, with IITA; undated).
- The IITA training manual originally listed in sources.txt (iita.org/wp-content/.../Growing_cassava_a_training_manual...)
  returned 404 and its CGSpace copy returned 429 (rate-limited), so this guide was used instead.
- Check: planting times per zone (rain forest Mar-Nov, derived savanna Apr-Aug, SGS May-Jul, NGS Jul-Aug)
  and fertiliser rates (forest and savanna) are from an old guide. Confirm with current ADP/NRCRI advice.
- Removed on purpose: the source recommends "D-Aldrex 20" (aldrin) for termites and "Gamalin 20" (lindane)
  for grasshoppers, and herbicide brands including paraquat. Aldrin and lindane are banned persistent organic
  pollutants; do not reintroduce them. Variety names (TMS 30572 etc.) are kept; check they are still recommended.

## rice-production.md
- URL: https://naerls.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Rice-Production-Processing-Utilization-and-Marketing-in-Nigeria.pdf
  (NAERLS Extension Bulletin, Rice Production, Processing, Utilization and Marketing in Nigeria).
- The IITA "Guide to Rice Production in Northern Nigeria" URL returned 404 and was not used.
- Check: the bulletin gives two different dibbling spacings (25 x 20 cm and 25 x 25 cm); the file uses 25 x 20 cm.
- Check: the bulletin gives a general three-split nitrogen plan (40/30/30%) as well as the per-ecology plans used
  in the file. Confirm which to give farmers.
- Removed on purpose: carbofuran, paraquat (Gramoxone), seed-dressing and storage-insecticide brand names and
  fumigant rates. cypermethrin and lambda-cyhalothrin were kept as named in the bulletin.
- Variety names (FARO 44, 52, 55, 56, 58, 59, 60, 61) are kept; check they are still recommended.

## sorghum-production.md
- URL: https://oar.icrisat.org/11531/1/Handbook%20on%20Sorghum%20production%20for%20NE%20printed%20version.pdf
  (ICRISAT Handbook on Improved Agronomic Practices for Sorghum Production in North East Nigeria, ~2020).
- Applies to North East Nigeria (Borno, Adamawa). Planting and harvest months are for those states only.
- Check: mancozeb as seed dressing (named in the source). Insecticides (cypermethrin, carbaryl) were left out.
- Check: SAMSORG variety list and maturity periods.

## grain-storage-aflatoxin.md
- URLs:
  - https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/6419854d-6c09-4668-b1f4-f79533c7e6d5/content
    (IITA/AgResults 2019, Farmers' Guide to Management of Aflatoxin in Maize and Groundnuts in Africa: trainer's guide,
    handle 10568/107314)
  - https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/172541d0-2c50-4bbf-bafb-b84b8843e656/content
    (IITA/AgResults 2019, Farmers' Guide to Management of Aflatoxins in Maize in Africa, handle 10568/107313)
  - IITA maize guide (Wayback URL above) for storage moisture and weevils.
- The Feed the Future "Manual for Maize Handling and Storage" (canr.msu.edu) was blocked by the site's bot
  protection and was not used.
- Check: the file names Aflasafe (as the IITA guide does) without any rate, and tells farmers to ask the ADP
  if it is approved and available. Verify current NAFDAC registration and the label before keeping this.
- The sources do not describe the health effects of aflatoxin, so the file does not either. Add a health
  source if farmers need that explained.

## nimet-seasonal-prediction-2026.md
- URLs:
  - https://nimet.gov.ng/downloader?file=admin/uploads/publications/698da9653c517_SCP%202026%20Summary%20for%20Policy%20makers_.pdf&id=76
    (NiMet 2026 SCP Summary for Policy Makers, Feb 2026; page https://nimet.gov.ng/publication_detail?id=76)
  - https://nimet.gov.ng/downloader?file=admin/uploads/publications/6a7e1e83de1c0_NiMet%20Seasonal%20Climate%20Prediction%20Update.pdf&id=92
    (NiMet SCP Update: August 2026 and ASO 2026 Rainfall Forecast; page https://nimet.gov.ng/publication_detail?id=92)
- The 2026 SCP keynote PDF (scp.nimet.gov.ng) returned 404 and was not used.
- Time-sensitive: by October 2026 the season is nearly over. Replace this file when the 2027 SCP is released
  (usually early in the year) and check for any NiMet update after August 2026.
- Check: the August update says a strong El Niño may affect the end and length of the season in the north,
  so the February cessation dates may no longer hold. The update forecasts wetter-than-normal ASO in the north
  but also says El Niño history suggests reduced rainfall in the north; the file reports the forecast only.
- Many pages of the SCP are maps (images) with no extractable text; state/LGA tables in maps were not used.
