Business names in Nigeria are registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) online, on the CAC Company Registration Portal (iCRP) at https://icrp.cac.gov.ng. You must create an account on the portal before you can register or manage a business name. The portal is for registration; the official CAC website is www.cac.gov.ng.

Registering a business name has two parts. First you reserve the name and get an AV Code (Approval Validation Code). Then you use that AV Code to register the business.

Steps to reserve a business name on the iCRP, from the CAC user guide and FAQ:
1. Go to https://icrp.cac.gov.ng and click Login.
2. Enter your username (or email or phone number) and password.
3. Enter the one-time code (OTP) sent to your registered email address. This is called two-factor authentication (2FA).
4. On your dashboard, click "Name Reservation".
5. Choose the Business Classification "Business Name" and the Business Type, for example Sole Proprietorship.
6. Type your proposed name and choose the Nature and Specific Nature of Business.
7. Click "Check Availability". If the name is not available, click "Update Business Name" or "Get Name Suggestions" to try another name.
8. Click "Continue to Payment" and pay through Remita.
9. Once payment is successful, the name is approved instantly and you receive your AV Code.

Steps to register the business name after reservation:
1. On your dashboard, click "Registration", then "Register New Business".
2. Enter your AV Code and click Continue.
3. Fill in the email address, business address and date of commencement, then click Save & Continue.
4. Fill in the proprietor's (owner's) details.
5. For each proprietor, upload a means of identification, a passport photograph and a signature, in image format only. Click "Add Proprietor Document" to save.
6. Review everything on the Preview Page.
7. Click "Proceed to Payment" and pay through Remita.

CAC says business name registrations are now approved by AI and processed instantly. After you submit, check your status on the dashboard. "Pending" means the AI is still reviewing, so wait a few moments and refresh. If your application is queried, click Action, then View Query History, then Modify Application to correct it and submit again. When it is approved, click Action, then Download Certificate and Status Report.

Some names need consent, for example names with restricted words. On the iCRP these go through a separate "Name Requiring Consent" process. CAC says these are still reviewed manually by an Approving Officer, and you get an email when the request is approved or queried.

If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page and follow the email you receive. If you are not sure which email is linked to your account, click "Check Email" on the login page. To change the email on your account, CAC says to write to helpdesk@cac.gov.ng or ict@cac.gov.ng with your account details.

Accredited agents are persons or institutions certified by CAC to file registrations for individuals and businesses. CAC's FAQ suggests using an accredited agent if you are in doubt. If you use an agent, make sure the agent is CAC-accredited.

Annual returns for business names are also filed on the iCRP. The iCRP home page says annual returns filing is available for business names registered before July 2025, and that an automatic AI-powered annual returns process is coming for both older and newer business names.
