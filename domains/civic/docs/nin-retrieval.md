If you enrolled for a NIN but forgot the number, you can get it back with the official NIMC USSD code *346#. You must dial it from the same phone number you used when you enrolled. NIMC says the code works on all networks and costs N20 (twenty naira), taken from your phone credit. This fee is stated on the NIMC website and in the NIMC FAQs 2026.

Steps to retrieve your NIN by USSD, from the NIMC website:
1. Dial *346# on the phone number you enrolled with.
2. From the options shown, type 1 to choose "NIN Retrieval".
3. Follow the steps on your screen and give the details asked for.

You can also retrieve your NIN by going to the nearest NIMC office or NIMC enrolment centre.

If you enrolled but have not received your NIN slip, NIMC says you should take your Tracking ID to the enrolment partner (FEP) or to the nearest NIMC office, so they can give you your NIN slip. Do not enrol again, because NIMC says a person who already enrolled does not need to re-enrol.

If your NIN has not yet been uploaded, the NIMC FAQs 2026 say to email NIMC customer care at nimccustomercare@nimc.gov.ng with your NIN. NIMC says the NIN will be uploaded within 72 hours after you contact customer care.

A NIN slip can be re-issued if it is lost, damaged or stolen, or if your record was changed. For a lost or stolen slip, NIMC asks for a recent police report, a court affidavit for loss of documents, evidence of payment and a photo ID. For a damaged slip, bring the damaged slip and evidence of payment. After a change of record, bring the transaction slip from the modification and evidence of payment for each field changed. NIMC says the slip is re-issued immediately.

There is a fee for re-issuing a NIN slip, and it is paid only through Remita. NIMC's own pages give different amounts for this fee, so ask at the NIMC office or check the amount shown on Remita before you pay.

How to pay a NIMC fee on Remita:
1. Go to www.remita.net.
2. Click "Pay Billers" under "Bills & Purchases".
3. Type "National Identity Management Commission" and choose the service you want to pay for. The amount is filled in for you.
4. Enter your name and phone number, then submit.
5. Print the invoice and note your RRR (Remita Retrieval Reference).
6. Pay by card, bank branch, USSD, internet banking or wallet.

You can also print a NIN slip with a QR code yourself. The NIMC website says to log in at https://myportal.nimc.gov.ng using the UserID and a one-minute OTP from the NIMC Mobile ID app. If you do not have a smartphone, NIMC says you can dial *346*2*Your NIN# on any phone to get the UserID and OTP by SMS.

Warning: do not pay social media pages or "agents" to get your NIN or NIN slip for you. NIMC warns that fake pages collect money and personal details. Use only the official NIMC website https://www.nimc.gov.ng. For help, email nimccustomercare@nimc.gov.ng or call the NIMC toll-free line 08000616462.
