Voter registration in Nigeria is done by the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) through the Continuous Voter Registration (CVR). INEC says any Nigerian citizen who is 18 years or older, lives or works in Nigeria, and has completed the CVR with biometric enrolment at an INEC registration centre can vote.

Registration is closed as of October 2026. The official CVR portal, https://cvr.inecnigeria.org, says: "The Continuous Voter Registration (CVR) exercise has ended." INEC says people who applied should expect an email when administrative processes are completed, and should follow the INEC website, X (Twitter) or Facebook for official announcements. BusinessDay reported an INEC statement that the third and final phase of the CVR ran from Monday, 11 May 2026 to Friday, 10 July 2026.

When registration is open, the INEC CVR portal (https://cvr.inecnigeria.org) offers these services:
1. New voter online registration or pre-registration, if you have never registered before. Pre-registration saves time when you visit the biometric data collection centre.
2. Find a registration centre at INEC state and local government offices and other locations.
3. Transfer your registration to another polling unit, if you are already a registered voter.
4. Update the information on your voter's card.
5. Replace a lost or damaged PVC.
6. Find where to pick up an uncollected PVC.

Even while registration is closed, a registered voter can still check the status of their PVC and find their collection centre on the CVR portal. To check if you are registered, use the INEC Voter Verification Service at https://cvr.inecnigeria.org/vvs. Enter your state and local government of registration, your last name, your first name and your date of birth.

PVC collection for 2026: news reports say INEC Chairman Prof. Joash Amupitan announced Friday, October 9, 2026, as the start date for the nationwide collection of Permanent Voter Cards (PVCs) ahead of the 2027 general elections. Blueprint and Legit.ng both reported this date. To find where to collect your PVC, use the "PVC Pickup" option on the INEC CVR portal, https://cvr.inecnigeria.org.

You cannot vote without your PVC. INEC says: "NO PVC, NO VOTING!" INEC also says you cannot vote with an expired or temporary voter card. Only registered voters who physically have a valid Permanent Voter Card (PVC) that matches their biometric records on the BVAS device will be accredited to vote. Keep your PVC safe from loss or damage.

On election day, INEC says polling units open at 8:30 AM and accreditation and voting end at 2:30 PM. Every voter on the queue on or before 2:30 PM is entitled to vote. Bring your physical PVC. The BVAS device checks your fingerprint, and if that fails it uses facial recognition.

INEC asks voters to refuse vote buying and selling. Do not accept money, gifts or promises in exchange for your vote.

For help with voter registration or your PVC, INEC's CVR help page gives the email iccc@inec.gov.ng and the phone numbers 0700-2255-4632, 090-5085-8629 and 090-8444-4333.
