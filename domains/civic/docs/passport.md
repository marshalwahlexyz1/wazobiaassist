The Nigerian international passport is issued by the Nigeria Immigration Service (NIS). All citizens of Nigeria, whether by birth, descent, adoption, registration or naturalisation, are eligible for a Nigerian passport if they meet the conditions set by law. Citizens by naturalisation, registration, adoption and surrogacy can only process their passport applications at the NIS Headquarters, Abuja.

The ordinary passport for Nigerians is the green standard e-passport. NIS says it comes in three types: 32 pages valid for 5 years, 64 pages valid for 5 years, and 64 pages valid for 10 years. Official (blue) and diplomatic passports are for government officials and diplomats.

Passport fees: NIS announced new standard passport fees that took effect on 1st September, 2025. For applications made in Nigeria, the 32-page passport with 5-year validity costs N100,000 and the 64-page passport with 10-year validity costs N200,000. For Nigerians applying from abroad, the fees stayed at $150 for the 32-page 5-year passport and $230 for the 64-page 10-year passport. Check the fee shown on the official portal before you pay, in case it has changed.

Steps to apply for a fresh (first) passport inside Nigeria, according to NIS:
1. Go to the official portal https://passport.immigration.gov.ng and choose "Apply for Fresh Passport".
2. Fill the passport application form online.
3. Upload your NPC birth certificate or a declaration of age from the high court, and your state of origin certificate.
4. Make payment online and book an appointment at the passport office you choose.
5. On your appointment date, go in person to that passport office with your payment and appointment slips.
6. Your photograph and fingerprints (biometrics) are captured at the office.

Documents for an adult (18 years and above) who is a citizen by birth, as listed by NIS: a letter of identification from your local government or state of origin, or your National Identity Card (government workers may use a letter of introduction with their staff ID); the completed application form and 2 passport photographs endorsed at the back by your guarantor; a guarantor's form signed by a Commissioner for Oaths, with a copy of the guarantor's passport data page and one photo of the guarantor; your marriage certificate if you are married; your birth certificate or age declaration; and the acknowledgement slip and evidence of payment.

Documents for a child under 18: the completed application form with 1 passport photograph endorsed on the back by the consenting parent; evidence of the parent's Nigerian citizenship; a birth certificate (issued by the National Population Commission for children born in Nigeria); a letter of consent from either parent; and the acknowledgement slip and evidence of payment. An adopted child also needs a court order and a letter of approval from the State Ministry of Women and Child Development.

To renew a passport, whether expired or not, apply online at https://passport.immigration.gov.ng, upload your NPC birth certificate or declaration of age, state of origin certificate and passport data page, pay and book an appointment. Then go to the passport office on your appointment date with your payment and appointment slips and your old passport booklet for photo and fingerprint capture.

To correct an error or change data (for example after marriage), apply online and upload the documents NIS lists, such as your birth certificate or declaration of age, an application letter giving the reason, a newspaper publication, a court affidavit, your state of origin certificate, your passport data page, and a marriage certificate for a change of marital status. Then attend your appointment with your current passport booklet.

On the portal you can also check the earliest available appointment date at each processing centre, test your passport photo before applying, resume an unfinished application, and check your application status or print your receipt. Nigerians abroad should apply at the nearest Nigerian Embassy, High Commission or Consulate.

Avoid touts and middlemen. NIS says the enhanced e-passport process is automated to address touting and middlemen, and payment is strictly online. Apply and pay only through the official portal https://passport.immigration.gov.ng.
