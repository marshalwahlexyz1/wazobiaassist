All mobile network operators in Nigeria have been directed to update their subscriber records so that each subscriber's National Identification Number (NIN) is captured. Linking your NIN to your SIM card is done with your network provider. The Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC) says linking your mobile number to your NIN gives a way of verifying your identity and protects both your identity and your mobile line.

Steps to link your NIN to your SIM, according to the NCC:
1. Confirm your NIN, either from your NIN enrolment slip or by dialling *346# from the phone number you used for NIN enrolment.
2. Submit your NIN to your network provider through the online, USSD or other channels they provide.
3. Your network provider checks your NIN on the NIMC database and links the SIM to your NIN if the check is successful. You will be told whether the linking worked or if more details are needed.

The code *996# works on all networks for linking your NIN to your SIM. Both NIMC and the NCC give this code. Dial *996# and follow the steps. The NCC also says you can dial *996*NIN# (put your 11-digit NIN in place of "NIN") to submit your NIN for linking.

Network websites for NIN linking, as listed on the NIMC website. You can also dial *996# on any of these networks, or visit a service centre of your network.
1. Airtel: https://airtel.com.ng/NIN_information
2. MTN: https://nin.mtn.ng/
3. Glo: https://www.gloworld.com/ng/nin/
4. 9mobile: https://www.9mobile.com.ng/nin

If you have more than one phone number, you use the same NIN for all of them, because your NIN is your one unique identity number. But you must submit your NIN to each network provider for each line, so that every SIM is linked.

You do not need to be physically present to link your NIN. The NCC says subscribers can link their NIN to their SIM through the online and USSD channels provided by their network provider.

Forgotten your NIN? Dial *346# from the phone number you enrolled with and choose "NIN Retrieval" (type 1). NIMC says this costs N20, deducted from your phone credit. You can also visit a NIMC office near you.

The NCC says the linking of SIM to NIN is free. Beware of fake links. The NCC says only the links and channels given by mobile network providers, NIMC or the NCC are genuine. Do not submit your NIN through any link or channel that did not come from your network provider, NIMC or the NCC.
