The Bank Verification Number (BVN) is a unique number given to every bank customer in Nigeria. It is run by the Nigeria Inter-Bank Settlement System (NIBSS). NIBSS says the BVN gives you one identity that is accepted across ALL Nigerian banks, not just one bank, and it is linked to every account you have in any Nigerian bank. It helps protect your accounts from unauthorised access and identity theft.

Steps to get a BVN, according to NIBSS:
1. Walk into any branch of your bank.
2. Fill and submit the BVN Enrolment form.
3. Present yourself for data capture. Your fingerprints and facial image are taken.
4. You are given an acknowledgment slip with a transaction ID.
5. Within 24 hours the system confirms your application, your BVN is generated, and you receive an SMS.

What to bring: NIBSS says you must submit an acceptable means of identification for BVN enrolment. All ten fingers and your facial image are captured. If you need to update your BVN information later, NIBSS says this is done physically at your bank branch.

How to check your BVN by phone: dial *565*0# from the phone number registered for your BVN, and NIBSS shows your BVN. NIBSS says a service fee of N20 is charged. No BVN will be returned if your phone number is linked to more than one BVN.

How to confirm that a BVN is correct: dial *565*1#, enter the BVN and send. NIBSS returns the name registered to that BVN in the USSD session. A service fee of N20 is charged.

Nigerians living abroad can get a BVN without visiting a bank in Nigeria, through the NIBSS Non-Resident BVN (NRBVN) service on nrbvn.com. NIBSS says you register online by submitting a valid ID document (such as a passport or national ID card), proof of where you live, and other KYC documents, and you go through a liveness check. NRBVN also lets you open accounts with partner banks in Nigeria.

Protect your BVN. NIMC warns: do not submit your NIN or BVN to unauthorised apps, websites or social media pages. NIMC says it will never ask you to upload your BVN or NIN on any unauthorised portal. Be careful of messages from fake email addresses asking you to click links and upload your BVN. NIBSS says BVN enrolment is done at a branch of your bank, or through NRBVN if you live abroad.
