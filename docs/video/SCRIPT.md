# Demo video script (about 4 minutes)

Slides in this folder (1920×1080): `title.png` (opening), `evidence.png` (numbers, 12 Oct 2026), `closing.png` (roadmap and links).

**Before recording:** ask every question below once to check it works. Phone on Do Not Disturb, notifications off, browser in light mode at about 125% zoom. A native Hausa and a native Yorùbá speaker should say the voice questions, and check their wording first.

**Recording:** Win + Alt + R records a browser window on Windows (or use OBS). Use the phone's built-in screen recorder for Telegram.
**Editing:** Clipchamp (built into Windows) or CapCut. Cut or speed up the waiting while the bot thinks. Add English subtitles for the Hausa and Yorùbá lines.
**Export:** 1080p, under 5 minutes. Upload to YouTube (Unlisted) or Google Drive (anyone with the link can view), and open the link in a private window before submitting.
**Avoid** recording between 12:20 and 12:45 PM US Eastern time (5:20–5:45 PM in Nigeria) on 12 Oct: the bot restarts then.
