# User acquisition plan

## Goal

**1,000 unique users within 90 days of the award**, each asking at least one real question. A user counts once however many questions they ask. We start from a working system and a tested audience: in the challenge we logged 170 real interactions on the web and Telegram, 73% by voice, 97% of rated answers helpful.

| Day | Unique users | Also tracked |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | 250 | 30%+ of questions in Hausa, Igbo or Yorùbá |
| 60 | 600 | 30%+ of questions by voice |
| 90 | 1,000 | 70%+ of rated answers 👍; 300+ users who return with a second question |

The bot's anonymous log gives these numbers every Monday, by channel, language and topic.

## The funnel

These are estimates we check in week 2 and adjust.

| Channel | Calculation | Users |
|---|---|---|
| Partner WhatsApp groups | 40 groups × ~150 members × 10% who try it | ~600 |
| In-person demo sessions | 20 sessions × 25 attendees, half ask on the spot | ~250 |
| Community radio call-ins | 6 segments × 20–40 new users | ~180 |
| Campus ambassadors | 6 ambassadors × 25 verified new users | 150 |
| Referrals | ~10% of users bring one more person after a 👍 answer | ~100 |
| **Total** | | **~1,280** |

The 28% margin over 1,000 covers channels that underperform.

## Before launch (week 0)

- **WhatsApp Business.** The WhatsApp channel is already built. We move from the Twilio sandbox to a Meta-verified WhatsApp Business number named "WazobiaAssist", so users message one number with no join code. WhatsApp is where most Nigerians already are, so it becomes the main channel.
- **Always-on server** with an uptime check every 5 minutes and alerts; target 99% uptime.
- **One QR card** for the web page, Telegram and WhatsApp.
- **Invite kit in four languages**, checked by native speakers: a three-line text, a 30-second voice note and the QR card as an image for WhatsApp Status.
- **Measurement:** a one-tap "How did you hear about us?" on first use, a referral code for every partner and ambassador, and a "share with a friend" message after every 👍.

## Month 1 (days 1–30): partners and launch, 250 users

- **Five anchor partners, one per user group:** a market traders' association (Lagos or Ibadan), a farmers' cooperative linked to a state Agricultural Development Programme (Kano or Kaduna), a students' union at a federal university, a primary health centre with a weekly antenatal clinic, and an NYSC Community Development Service group.
- **Each partner** shares the invite in 8 of its WhatsApp groups (40 groups in total), hosts two 30-minute demos and gives feedback after two weeks.
- **10 demo sessions.** A 5-minute demo on a phone, then everyone asks one real question on their own phone while we help with the first message. People who have asked once in person come back.
- **Team networks.** Each team member personally invites 30 people (family, places of worship, alumni groups) with the voice note in their language.
- **Every Monday** we review the 10 most-asked unanswered questions and every 👎 answer, and fix the top issues that week.

## Month 2 (days 31–60): local-language reach, 600 users

- **Community radio.** Six call-in segments of 10–15 minutes, two per language, on regional stations such as Freedom Radio Kano (Hausa), Fresh FM Ibadan (Yorùbá) and Dream FM Enugu (Igbo). The presenter asks WazobiaAssist listeners' questions live and reads out the WhatsApp number twice. Radio reaches the people who are least served by English-only websites.
- **Campus ambassadors.** Six students, two per language, for six weeks, running demo tables in departments and hostels. Paid ₦30,000 a month plus ₦200 for each new user who asks a question, counted by referral code.
- **10 more demo sessions**, each on one topic: "Know your rights with the police", "Is your drug registered?", "NIN and BVN without stress".
- **Eight short videos** of 30–60 seconds for TikTok, Instagram Reels and WhatsApp Status, each a real question and answer in one language, for example checking a NAFDAC number in Hausa.

## Month 3 (days 61–90): referrals and institutions, 1,000 users

- **Deadline campaigns.** Send the right topic to partner groups when people need it: PVC registration, JAMB registration, planting season and immunisation days.
- **Public-body pilot.** Offer a state ICT agency, a local government information office or the Legal Aid Council a free WazobiaAssist link for their own channels, with a monthly report of what their audience asked.
- **New topics** from the log's most-requested gaps, likely birth certificates (NPC) and driver's licences (FRSC), so returning users find new answers.
- **Feature-phone test.** A small USSD or voice-call pilot with one partner community, for users without smartphones.

## How we report and adjust

- **Weekly:** unique users, new users by channel, questions by language and topic, share of voice, share of 👍, median response time and unanswered questions.
- **Days 30, 60 and 90:** a one-page report to NAIC with these numbers and three anonymised user stories.
- **If a month ends more than 20% below target,** we move budget from the weakest channel to the strongest and add five more demo sessions.

## Why we can reach 1,000

The product already works end to end on N-ATLaS, by voice, in four languages, and real users rated 97% of answers helpful. The plan puts it where people already are (WhatsApp groups, radio, campuses, clinics and markets), in their own language, through organisations they trust, and measures every channel every week so the budget follows what works.
